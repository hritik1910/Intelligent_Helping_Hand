# Sample SPI Inspection Procedure

Document type: Fictional demonstration SOP

## Purpose
This procedure describes initial checks when an SPI solder-paste measurement is outside its expected range.

## Initial checks
1. Confirm that the correct PCB product and inspection recipe are selected.
2. Review the SPI measurement report and identify the affected board and pad locations.
3. Compare the measurements with the approved limits for the selected product.
4. Check for visible stencil contamination or damage in accordance with the authorised work instruction.
5. Verify the printer setup and process settings against the approved job recipe.

## Escalation
If the deviation repeats or remains outside the approved limits, follow the site's product-hold and escalation process and notify the responsible process engineer.

Do not change approved inspection limits or machine settings without authorisation.

## Document limitation
This is a fictional learning document. It is not an OEM-approved work instruction and must not be used to operate real manufacturing equipment.
